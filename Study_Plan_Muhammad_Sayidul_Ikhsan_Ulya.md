## 2. Study Direction and Learning Plan

My proposed study direction is:

**Smart Manufacturing and AI-Supported Production Process Improvement**

This direction reflects my interest in understanding how digital technologies and artificial intelligence may support manufacturing activities. I am particularly interested in how production records, technical documentation, data analysis, and digital workflows can help identify inefficiencies and support quality improvement. However, I understand that effective manufacturing depends first on a solid understanding of materials, machinery, technical design, production methods, and measurement. Therefore, my priority will be to develop the necessary manufacturing fundamentals before exploring how my previous IT and AI-related learning can be applied in this field.

Before and during the early stage of my studies, I plan to strengthen my technical English vocabulary and prepare myself to learn the fundamentals of precision design and manufacturing. I want to develop an understanding of technical drawing, computer-aided design, materials, manufacturing processes, precision measurement, industrial safety, and quality control. Since these subjects represent a new technical direction for me, I will approach them systematically. I plan to review learning materials regularly, prepare personal summaries, practice technical concepts, and seek guidance from lecturers and classmates when I encounter difficulties. My goal is not only to complete the required courses, but also to understand how products are designed, manufactured, measured, and evaluated.

After establishing this foundation, I plan to explore the relationship between manufacturing processes and digital technologies. My previous experience in web development and independent study of data-related topics has helped me understand the value of structured information, consistent records, and clear workflows. I hope to explore how these principles may support production documentation, inspection records, quality monitoring, and process analysis. I am also interested in learning how AI algorithms could assist with information analysis, research, and identifying possible opportunities for workflow improvement. At the same time, I recognize that AI-generated results may be inaccurate and cannot independently guarantee manufacturing precision. Any practical application must therefore be evaluated through reliable data, appropriate technical methods, real-world verification, and human supervision.

In the later stage of the program, I hope to participate in practical projects, laboratory activities, and industry-related learning opportunities available through the university. I want to develop a clearer understanding of real manufacturing challenges, such as production defects, process variation, inconsistent quality, and inefficient workflows. If suitable opportunities are available, I would like to undertake a practical project connecting manufacturing knowledge with digital workflow improvement or data-supported quality control. Through such work, I hope to learn how to identify a problem, gather relevant information, evaluate possible solutions, and determine whether an improvement is practical. I also plan to maintain a personal learning portfolio containing technical summaries, project documentation, and records of my progress. This portfolio will help me evaluate my development and demonstrate how I have integrated my previous digital skills with newly acquired manufacturing knowledge.

I plan to build my skills from the basics through regular vocabulary practice, listening exercises, reading activities, and everyday conversation. As my proficiency develops, I will also learn vocabulary related to manufacturing, technical tools, workplace safety, quality control, and industrial communication.

I view Mandarin learning as part of my broader preparation for academic and professional life rather than only as a language milestone. My previous education and technical experience have taught me the importance of continuous learning and adapting to different challenges. I intend to strengthen these qualities throughout the program and remain open to feedback from lecturers and industry professionals. My goal is to become not only academically capable, but also professionally reliable and prepared to learn from practical experience.
