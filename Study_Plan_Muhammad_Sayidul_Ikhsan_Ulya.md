# Study Plan
## Cheng Shiu University
### Post-Baccalaureate Program in Semiconductor Smart Operations and Maintenance

## 1. Academic Background and Motivation

My academic background combines humanities-based research, technical education, and practical experience in software development. I hold a bachelor's degree in the humanities, during which I conducted thesis research on media representation and discourse analysis. This research developed my ability to examine information critically, interpret meaning, and approach a subject through systematic analysis. Although my university education was not in engineering, I continued to develop my technical interests through vocational education in Software Engineering and independent learning.

My technical learning has included data science, machine learning, data wrangling, and data visualization, supported by relevant training and certificates. I further applied my digital skills during my frontend development internship at Gamelab Educa Studio in Salatiga, Indonesia, from September to December 2023. My responsibilities included designing user interfaces for a company profile landing page for small businesses and a dashboard template using Figma, followed by implementation with HTML and CSS. I was also selected as the best performer of the week for website design and functionality. This experience strengthened my interest in developing practical digital solutions and organizing information through functional interfaces.

I now aim to extend my technical foundation into semiconductor smart operations and maintenance. I want to understand how semiconductor production systems and equipment operate, how operational conditions are monitored, and how maintenance and quality-related decisions are supported by technical information. My previous learning in software development, data analysis, and artificial intelligence provides a starting point for exploring how digital technologies can complement engineering practice. However, I recognize that applying these tools in semiconductor operations requires specialized technical knowledge, an understanding of industrial processes, and careful human supervision. Through the Post-Baccalaureate Program in Semiconductor Smart Operations and Maintenance at Cheng Shiu University, I hope to build this foundation and develop the ability to connect digital problem-solving skills with the practical requirements of advanced manufacturing.

## 2. Study Direction and Learning Plan

### Proposed Study Direction

**Connecting Software and Data Skills with Semiconductor Smart Operations and Maintenance**

My proposed study direction is to build the technical knowledge needed to understand semiconductor equipment and operational processes, then explore how data and digital tools can support monitoring, maintenance, and quality-related work. My existing background is primarily in software development and data-related learning, so I intend to approach this field by first developing a sound understanding of the underlying equipment and industrial processes.

### Before Starting the Program

Before beginning the program, I plan to review foundational concepts in electronics, basic electrical systems, industrial safety, equipment terminology, and data analysis. I will also strengthen my technical English vocabulary and begin or continue learning Mandarin to prepare for academic study, daily communication, and future workplace interaction in Taiwan. These are preparation goals rather than claims of existing expertise.

### Early Stage of Study

During the early stage of the program, I will prioritize the technical foundations required to understand semiconductor operations and maintenance. I aim to learn systematically from course materials, keep structured notes, review difficult topics, and seek clarification from lecturers and classmates when necessary. My goal is to understand the principles behind equipment operation, maintenance practices, and production processes rather than relying only on memorization.

### Later Stage of Study

After developing a stronger technical foundation, I hope to explore how software and data-related skills can complement equipment and operations knowledge. Depending on the courses and project opportunities available, I would like to investigate practical applications such as organizing maintenance records, visualizing equipment or process data, documenting operational conditions, and supporting the identification of irregularities. Any use of AI would need to be grounded in appropriate data, technical understanding, and human review. I will not assume that these applications are part of the formal curriculum unless confirmed by the university.

### Learning Outputs and Personal Development

Throughout the program, I intend to maintain a portfolio of learning outputs, such as technical summaries, data visualizations, structured documentation, and project reports where permitted and relevant. These materials would help me track my progress and demonstrate how I connect digital skills with newly acquired knowledge of semiconductor operations and maintenance.

## 3. Mandarin Adaptation and Professional Readiness

I understand that adapting to study and work in Taiwan involves more than academic learning. I plan to study Mandarin consistently, focusing first on everyday communication and gradually developing vocabulary relevant to education, equipment, safety, maintenance, and workplace interaction. I will confirm any formal language requirements or target levels with the university rather than assume a specific certification target.

I also intend to adapt to the expectations of an industrial environment by developing punctuality, careful documentation, teamwork, safety awareness, and attention to detail. I recognize that equipment-related work requires following procedures and communicating observations clearly. I will work to develop these habits alongside my technical studies.

## 4. Career Plan and Expected Contribution

After completing the program, I hope to pursue an entry-level role related to semiconductor operations support, equipment maintenance support, production documentation, process monitoring, or data-supported manufacturing operations, subject to my acquired qualifications and available opportunities. I understand that employment conditions and any post-graduation obligations must follow the applicable INTENSE Program agreement and official requirements.

In the longer term, I aim to combine my foundation in software development and data-related learning with specialized knowledge of semiconductor equipment and manufacturing processes. I would like to contribute to work that improves the organization and use of operational information, supports maintenance documentation, and helps teams interpret process data. I recognize that meaningful contributions in this field require continuous learning, practical experience, and collaboration with qualified engineering professionals.

In the future, I also hope that the skills I develop can be useful to advanced manufacturing development in Indonesia. My intention is to build the competence to connect digital problem-solving with real industrial needs, while remaining realistic about the technical knowledge and experience I must gain first.

## 5. Closing Statement

The Post-Baccalaureate Program in Semiconductor Smart Operations and Maintenance at Cheng Shiu University represents a potential pathway for me to expand from software development and data-related learning into a specialized industrial field. I understand that this transition will require sustained effort to acquire new technical foundations, adapt to a different academic environment, and develop the discipline expected in industrial operations.

I am prepared to approach the program as a learner, build my knowledge step by step, and explore responsible ways to connect digital tools with semiconductor operations and maintenance. Through this study, I hope to develop the interdisciplinary skills and practical understanding needed to contribute to advanced manufacturing over the course of my career.
